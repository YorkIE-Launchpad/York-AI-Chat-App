Executive summary

Bottom line

Onboarding is slow because customers wait on us, not because the product is hard to set up. Fixing data migration and the kickoff handoff would cut time to first value from 23 to about 12 days.

Brightline asked York IE to explain why new customers take more than three weeks to reach first value and why one in three never finish onboarding. We analysed 142 accounts onboarded between April and September 2026 and interviewed 11 customers.

Key findings

  • Data migration accounts for 9 of the 23 days. Customers wait on average nine days for a CSV import that the team processes by hand.
  • The sales-to-CS handoff loses context. 64% of kickoff calls repeat discovery questions the customer already answered during sales.
  • Mid-market accounts are hit hardest. They take 31 days on average, double the enterprise figure, because they get no named onboarding manager.

Recommendation

Ship self-serve data import, introduce a structured handoff document, and assign a pooled onboarding manager to mid-market accounts. Together these address roughly 80% of the delay at a one-time cost of about six engineering weeks.

Background and scope

Brightline sells scheduling software to outpatient clinics. Onboarding was redesigned in early 2025, but time to first value has crept up for three consecutive quarters while new account volume grew 40%.

Objective

Identify the main causes of onboarding delay and recommend changes that bring time to first value under 14 days by the end of Q1 2027.

1. Customers spend most of onboarding waiting on data migration

Of the 23 days, only six involve work by the customer. The largest single block is waiting for the implementation team to clean and import patient and staff lists.

We had everything ready on day two. Then we sent the spreadsheet and heard nothing for over a week. Practice manager, 6-location clinic group (interview, Oct 2026)

2. The handoff from sales repeats work the customer has already done

Kickoff calls average 75 minutes. In 64% of recorded calls, the CS manager asked for clinic count, staff roles or integration needs that were already in the deal notes.

Why this matters Each repeated discovery call pushes configuration back by roughly two days, because CS waits for answers before starting setup.
